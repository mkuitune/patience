Quoting the attribution from the above link:

These poker-sized playing cards were created exclusively from third-party public-domain and CC0 SVG vector assets (some of which were modified). No copyrighted or restricted commercial assets were used.

The designs follow the traditional Anglo-American (French-suited) playing-card conventions that appear in many commercially available decks. They aim for a clean, familiar look consistent with standard modern playing cards while remaining fully based on public-domain source material.

These cards are provided for free commercial and non-commercial use, including inclusion in video games, physical printing, and resale, with no attribution required.
